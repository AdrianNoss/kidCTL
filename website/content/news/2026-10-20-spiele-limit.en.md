---
title: "Game limit: separate time for gaming"
date: 2026-10-20
version: "0.25.0"
summary: "Games get their own daily allowance – the rest of the computer stays usable for homework."
---

Homework and games on the same computer? With the game limit you set, per weekday, how much of the screen time may
be spent on games. Steam, Heroic and everything in the "Games" group count as games.

Shortly before the limit the child gets a warning, then the game is closed – the computer itself stays usable. If
the last level needs a few more minutes, the child asks for more game time in the tray app and you decide from your
phone via push notification. The statistics also show which game ran for how long.
