date: 2026-11-20

summary: "Every page of the parent interface and the administration reviewed and smoothed out; the browser no longer applies any embedded styles."
---

We went through every page of the parent interface and the administration on phone and computer, in light and dark
mode, and fixed small rough edges:

- The remaining time on the overview no longer wraps onto two lines.
- Under "Recently blocked", the labels "locked" and "monitored only" are fully visible on phones again.
- The "Screen time per day" chart fits narrow screens without cutting off the last day.
- The sign-in page no longer shows a cryptic browser message when the passkey suggestion is not available.
- More consistent spacing for buttons and option fields.

On top of that, the **security policy (CSP)** is stricter: the browser only applies styling from the server's
stylesheet, injected styles are blocked. Nothing changes for you.
