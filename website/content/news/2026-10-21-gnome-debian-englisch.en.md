date: 2026-10-21

summary: "KidCTL now supports GNOME next to KDE Plasma, Debian and Ubuntu next to Arch – and speaks English and German."
---

KidCTL is no longer tied to one distribution:

- **GNOME** (Wayland) is supported next to KDE Plasma, both on the same computer if you like.
- **Debian 13 and Ubuntu 24.04/26.04** including derivatives like Kubuntu, Linux Mint and Pop!_OS get their own
  packages from a signed APT repository served by your server.
- **English:** the web interface, emails, push notifications and everything the child sees are now available in
  English and German. Parents choose their language in their profile; on the child's computer the desktop
  language is used.
