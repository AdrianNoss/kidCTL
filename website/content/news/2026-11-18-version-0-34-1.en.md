---
title: "Version 0.34.1: security update for the server"
date: 2026-11-18
version: "0.34.1"
summary: "A bug in the server's language switch is fixed. Please update your server."
---

Version 0.34.1 fixes a flaw in the server: a crafted link to the address of your KidCTL server could make the language
switch redirect to a foreign website. No data was accessible, but such a link could have been misused for phishing.
Versions 0.26.0 to 0.34.0 are affected, the severity is low.

Please update your server to 0.34.1 (rebuild the image and restart it). The agents on the children's computers are not
affected. Details are in the [security advisory](https://github.com/AdrianNoss/kidCTL/security/advisories).
