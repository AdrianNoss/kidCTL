summary: "KidCTL now has its own website with features, quick start, news and all releases – in English and German."

KidCTL now has its own project website. It shows the features with screenshots, explains the installation in a quick
start and lists every version with the Windows installer and its checksum.

News appear here with every new minor version and with security-relevant updates; the RSS feed makes sure you don't
miss any. The roadmap shows what is being worked on. Like the software itself, this site does without tracking,
cookies and external services.
