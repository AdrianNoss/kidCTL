title: "Version 0.47: School holidays, public holidays and blocked times"
date: 2026-11-23
version: "0.47.0"
summary: "School holidays and public holidays with their own schedule, blocked times like school or dinner, hours past midnight and games from Lutris and Bottles."
---

- **School holidays and public holidays:** Under *Settings → Holidays* you enter your family's school holiday periods
  and choose the region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland, per state or canton). Each child gets a separate school holiday
  schedule under *Times*. Public holidays count as a weekday of your choice, e.g. like Sunday. The server calculates
  public holidays itself, without fetching anything from outside.
- **Blocked times:** Up to three blocked times per day, such as school or dinner, lock the computer within the allowed
  hours – with a warning, just like at the end of the time.
- **Past midnight:** The allowed hours may run until 06:00 the next morning at the latest, e.g. from 16:00 to 01:00 at
  the weekend.
- **Lutris and Bottles:** On Linux, kidCTL recognises games from Lutris and Bottles and shows them with their names.
- **Chat:** If the child sends too many messages in a short time, the tray app shows a notice instead of silently
  dropping the message.

Blocked times and hours past midnight need kidCTL 0.47 on the children's computers – the easiest way is the
**Update** button on the device page. Until then, older versions only enforce the allowed hours up to 23:59.
