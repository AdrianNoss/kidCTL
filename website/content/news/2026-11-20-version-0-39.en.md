---
title: "Version 0.39: Signing in in two steps"
date: 2026-11-20
version: "0.39.0"
summary: "Signing in now asks for your password first and then for the code from your authenticator app."
---

Signing in is now split into two steps. First you enter your email address and password, then the code from your
authenticator app on the next page. This is clearer, and password managers fill in the first step more reliably.

Protection stays as strong as before: nobody gets into your account without the password **and** the code. You have
five minutes for the second step. This change prepares signing in with passkeys, which comes next.
