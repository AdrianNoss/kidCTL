---
title: "Version 0.33: open with the child, ready for a server move"
date: 2026-11-17
version: "0.33.0"
summary: "On first sign-in KidCTL tells the child what is recorded. Agents can later move to a new server without re-pairing."
---

Children should know where they stand: when a child signs in for the first time after being assigned, the tray app
shows once that the account is managed by KidCTL, what is recorded (screen time, programs and games used, blocked
programs and websites, chat) and what is not (website contents, typing, screen, files).

Version 0.33 also prepares two later steps: if the server moves to a new address, the child's computers switch on
their own – but only if the new server really knows them. And a future pause mode lifts all restrictions and shows
"KidCTL paused" instead of locking children out.

On the server, names, chat, activity log, statistics and email addresses are now encrypted with a separate key for
each family.
