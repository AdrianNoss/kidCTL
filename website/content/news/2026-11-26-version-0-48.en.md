---
title: "Version 0.48: Limits per app, learning apps and time bank"
date: 2026-11-26
version: "0.48.0"
summary: "A separate daily limit for the browser, chat or single apps, learning apps without screen time and a time bank for unused minutes."
---

- **Limit per app or category:** Under *Child → Times*, a single app or a category (*Browser*, *Chat*, *Music &
  video*) gets its own daily limit, up to 20 per child. Like the game limit, kidCTL warns 5 and 1 minute before and
  then closes only those apps; the rest of the computer stays usable. The time counts across all of the child's
  devices.
- **"Doesn't count":** Under *Child → Apps* you mark learning or office apps that don't use up screen time. While
  only such apps are running, the tray app shows "Your time is not running right now". Browsers, games, terminals and
  launchers can't be exempted.
- **Time bank:** If you like, part of the unused minutes moves to the next days, with a share, a maximum per day and
  an expiry that you set yourself.
- **Small things:** Apps on USB sticks and external drives can no longer be allowed. Blocked times on the Times page
  can be collapsed, the *Pair device* page links straight to the current Windows installer, and your own domain
  lists hold up to 1,000 entries.

Limits per app and "Doesn't count" need kidCTL 0.48 on the children's computers – the easiest way is the **Update**
button on the device page. The time bank also works with older versions.
