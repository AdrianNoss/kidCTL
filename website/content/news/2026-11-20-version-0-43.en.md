---
title: "Version 0.43: Sign in with a passkey"
date: 2026-11-20
version: "0.43.0"
summary: "Parents now sign in with their fingerprint, face or device PIN – without password and authenticator code."
---

From now on you can sign in with a **passkey**: your fingerprint, face or the PIN of your phone or computer is enough,
no password and no code from the authenticator app. Passkeys are secure because they are bound to your KidCTL address
and cannot be phished.

How it works: in your **profile** under "Passkeys", briefly confirm that it is you, then "Add passkey". Ideally create
one on your phone and one on your computer. You get an email for every new or deleted passkey.

Password and authenticator remain available. If you use "Forgot password" or set up the authenticator again, create
your passkeys again afterwards.
