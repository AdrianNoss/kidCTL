---
title: "Version 0.38: KidCTL with the Windows 11 look"
date: 2026-11-19
version: "0.38.0"
summary: "On Windows, the KidCTL app now looks like Windows 11 – light or dark, in the child's accent colour."
---

On Windows computers, KidCTL now matches the look of Windows 11. Windows, menu and requests follow the light or dark
mode and the accent colour set on the computer. The taskbar icon is simply the KidCTL symbol with a coloured dot for
the time status. The remaining time appears when you hover over it and at the top of the menu.

Notifications show the KidCTL logo, and the installer is tidier: server address and pairing code are listed one
below the other. On Linux, everything stays as it was.
