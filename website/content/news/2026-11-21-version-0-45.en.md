---
title: "Version 0.45: Invoices with your logo"
date: 2026-11-21
version: "0.45.0"
summary: "For operators with billing: new invoices show your service's logo and name from the appearance settings at the top."
---

If you run kidCTL with billing, newly issued invoices now show the **logo** and the **name of your service** from
*Administration → Appearance* in the top left corner. Without a logo, only the name is shown. Sender details stay as
before.

Invoices already issued do not change – their PDF stays exactly as it was issued.
