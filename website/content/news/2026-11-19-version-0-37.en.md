title: "Version 0.37: Consistent emails"
date: 2026-11-19
version: "0.37.0"
summary: "All KidCTL emails now share the same tidy layout – with logo, light and dark, and still with a plain-text version."
---

Confirmations, password resets, invitations, invoices, usage reports or news: all emails now look the same. They show
the logo at the top, actions as clear buttons and adapt to the light or dark mode of your mail app. Every email still
includes a plain-text version. No images are loaded from the web, and there is no tracking.

The bottom of every email says why you received it. It also links to news and guides and, for operators serving
several families, shows their details with imprint and privacy policy.

For operators: enter your provider details under "Appearance". Under "Emails" you can preview every email type and send
yourself a test. You format newsletters with simple Markdown and see a preview before sending.
