---
title: "New web interface in Material 3 style"
date: 2026-11-15
summary: "The parents' web interface has been redesigned: built for the phone, with light and dark mode."
---

Most decisions are made on the go, on the phone – so the parents' web interface is now built for it. It follows
Material Design 3: a navigation bar at the bottom (on the left on large screens), one card per child with the
remaining time and the most important actions, and a large remaining-time display on the "Today" page.

A child's page is organised in tabs: Today, Times, Internet, Apps, Statistics, Activity log and Chat. Light and dark
follow the system or can be set permanently. The report email has the new look too.
