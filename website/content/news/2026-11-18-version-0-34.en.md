---
title: "Version 0.34: invite parents, take your data with you"
date: 2026-11-18
version: "0.34.0"
summary: "Other parents join via an invitation link, and all of a family's data can be exported and imported on another server."
---

Adding another parent now works by invitation: under "Parents" you enter an email address, the invited person gets a
link and sets their own password and authenticator. Nobody has to make up a password for someone else anymore.

Under "Settings" you can give your family a name and export all of its data as a ZIP – children, rules, devices,
usage, log and chat, readable and with a format version. On another KidCTL server the export can be imported into a
new, empty family. This is the way for a later move between your own server and the hosted version.

For operators of several families there is a new administration area – with sign-up by invitation code, trial period,
support tickets and invoices. The hosted service itself starts later; it is on the roadmap.
