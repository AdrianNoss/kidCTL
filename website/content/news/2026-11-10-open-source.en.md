---
title: "KidCTL is open source (AGPL-3.0)"
date: 2026-11-10
summary: "New license: KidCTL is now under the GNU Affero General Public License – free to use, audit and modify."
---

KidCTL is now licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** (AGPL-3.0-or-later) instead of a
non-commercial-only license. That makes KidCTL real free software: anyone may read, audit, change and share the
source code.

For parental control this matters: you can check for yourself what runs on your children's computers and that no
data leaves your home. If you run a modified server for others, you must offer them its source code – the "Source
code" link in the web interface can point to your own fork for that.
