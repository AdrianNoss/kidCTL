---
title: "Version 0.35: your own branding and news by email"
date: 2026-11-18
version: "0.35.0"
summary: "Operators can offer KidCTL with their own name, logo and app icon and tell families about what's new – only if the family agrees."
---

If you run KidCTL for several families, you can now give it your own look: name, logo and app icon apply to the web
interface, the app on the phone, push notifications and all emails. "powered by KidCTL" is always shown below. The
programs on the children's computers are still called KidCTL.

Also new is a newsletter for news such as new versions and features. It only arrives if your family agrees – at
sign-up or under "Settings → News by email". It is off by default, every email has an unsubscribe link, and each
parent gets it in their language.

Also: on the phone, the bottom bar shows the "Support" entry in full again, and operators now manage their password,
authenticator and sessions themselves in their profile.
