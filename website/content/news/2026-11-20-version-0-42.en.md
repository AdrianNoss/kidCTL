---
title: "Version 0.42: New tray icon on Linux"
date: 2026-11-20
version: "0.42.0"
summary: "On Linux, KidCTL now shows the same simple icon as on Windows – with a coloured dot for the time status."
---

The KidCTL icon in the panel now looks the same on Linux as on Windows: the KidCTL symbol with a coloured dot (green,
yellow, red). It adapts to a light or dark panel. The remaining time appears when you hover over it and at the top of
the menu; warnings and the countdown before sign-out work as before.

On GNOME, the panel shows no tooltip – there the remaining time is at the top of the icon's menu.
