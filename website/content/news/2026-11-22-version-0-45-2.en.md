Version 0.45.2 fixes a gap in the **web filter on Linux**: with SafeSearch turned on, a blocked domain could under
certain circumstances still be reachable. Please update the agents on the child computers to 0.45.2. Details are in
the [security advisory](https://github.com/AdrianNoss/kidCTL/security/advisories/GHSA-6gh6-9rhc-2x2w).

Also:

- **Programs:** A child's program list shows programs as tiles; games are grouped under "Games".
- **Invoices:** The buttons in the invoice view are aligned properly.
- **Windows:** The tray app shows the KidCTL icon in the title bar and taskbar.
