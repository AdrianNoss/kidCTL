## In short

This site sets **no cookies**, uses **no tracking or analytics** and loads **nothing from other servers** (fonts and
icons are part of the site). There are no forms.

## Controller

Adrian Noss, address and contact see [imprint](../imprint/).

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA).
When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your **IP address**, the time and the
requested address, and logs them for the security of the service. I have no access to these logs; I only see
aggregated numbers (page views, downloads).

- Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR – legitimate interest in delivering the site securely and reliably.
- Transfer to the USA: GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- Details: [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Links to GitHub

Links to the repository, releases and downloads lead to github.com, where GitHub's privacy statement applies.

## The KidCTL software

The software itself only talks to the KidCTL server you run yourself – no telemetry, no connection to the
developers. See the [privacy note in the repository](https://github.com/AdrianNoss/kidCTL/blob/main/docs/code-signing.md#privacy).

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection
(Art. 15–21 GDPR) and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. As I don't store any
personal data of visitors myself, please contact GitHub directly regarding their server logs.
